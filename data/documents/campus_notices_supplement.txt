# Convocation and Degree Clearance Procedure
Graduating students of 8th semester must complete the University Clearance Form from the Central Library, Departmental Labs, Hostel Provost Office, and Treasurer Office before applying for their Final Transcript and Provisional Certificate. The Annual University Convocation is held in November each year; registration for the convocation opens in October at the Controller of Examinations Office (Block A, Room A-112) with a convocation gown and registration fee of Rs. 3,500.

# Summer Semester Registration Rules
The university offers an optional 8-week intensive Summer Semester during June and July primarily for students who wish to repeat failed ('F') or low-grade ('D', 'C-') courses or clear deficiency courses. A student may register for a maximum of 2 courses (up to 8 credit hours) during the Summer Semester. Regular new core courses cannot be taken in the Summer Semester without special permission from the Dean of Computing.
